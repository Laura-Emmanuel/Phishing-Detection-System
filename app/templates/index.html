    Detection Signals

    Brand impersonationFlags fake domains that mention trusted names outside their official domains.
    Credential trapsLooks for login, verify, password, billing, recovery, and account wording in risky places.
    URL structureChecks IP-based links, extra redirects, deep paths, query strings, subdomains, hyphens, and risky TLDs.
    Email sender validationChecks sender format, domain mismatch, free-mail senders, and brand impersonation.
    Sensitive information requestsFlags password, OTP, payment, banking, card, and recovery phrase requests.
    Email link inspectionExtracts URLs inside messages and runs the phishing URL checks against them.

    Random ForestPrimary model for structured website features.
    Logistic RegressionAlternative model for comparison and simpler scoring.
    No extra classifiersThe app filters saved artifacts so only these two algorithms are exposed.
